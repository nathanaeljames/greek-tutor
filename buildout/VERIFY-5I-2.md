## D. THE SOAK — the one thing only time can settle

- [ ] **D1. The half-screen modal, over days of ordinary use.** This is
      the item to keep an eye on for the rest of the cohort.
      What was found, and it is worth knowing because it explains why
      the last fix did not hold: the existing guard compared the visual
      viewport against `window.innerHeight` and assumed the iOS keyboard
      does not shrink `innerHeight`. That is true in Safari's tab UI and
      **false in a home-screen PWA**, which is the only way this app is
      used — so a stale reading shrank both numbers together and the
      guard saw nothing wrong with half a screen. The app now (a) also
      re-measures on window `focus` and on the Page Lifecycle `resume`,
      which is what an iOS screenshot actually delivers, (b) refuses,
      for 600 ms after any resume, a height far below the last one it
      published itself, and (c) re-measures four times across 400 ms
      instead of once, because a resumed viewport is still moving.
      *What to do:* use the app normally. **Take screenshots freely,
      including on pages with no modal**, then open a Hint. If a modal
      ever opens at half height again, note what you did in the minute
      before it — especially whether you had just typed in a speller.
      Also try: rotate to landscape and back, then open a modal; and
      leave the app backgrounded overnight, reopen, and open a modal.
