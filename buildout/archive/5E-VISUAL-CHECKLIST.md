# 5E-VISUAL-CHECKLIST.md — page-by-page comparison, chapters 4 and 5

Copy this to `5E-VISUAL-CHECKLIST-{ME}.md` and fill EVERY row. This is
the evidence for 5E-SPEC1 sec 0; an unfilled row counts as "not
verified", never as "passed".

Method, per row: load the page in a real browser at **320px** and at
**768px**, screenshot it to
`buildout/screenshots/5e-spec1-{me}/`, and put it side by side with the
corresponding screenshot in ch4railwalk.pdf / ch5railwalk.pdf. Then
record a verdict.

Verdicts:

- **MATCH** — the port renders what the original renders. Line breaks,
  indentation, emphasis, list markers, hanging indents, citation
  alignment, chart column alignment, merged chart rows, button sets,
  and which words are tappable all agree.
- **DIFFERS** — say WHAT differs, in one line. A DIFFERS row is not a
  failure; an unreported difference is.
- **BLOCKED** — could not evaluate. Say why.

Two reminders. A HAND CURSOR in a rail-walk screenshot marks a
clickable element; the marking is deliberate but NOT exhaustive, so its
absence proves nothing. And where the spec and a screenshot disagree,
the spec wins — record it as MATCH-TO-SPEC with a note, not as DIFFERS.

Columns: **320** and **768** take the verdict; **Shot** takes the
screenshot filename; **Notes** takes anything a grader would want.
